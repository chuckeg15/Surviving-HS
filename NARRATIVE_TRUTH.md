# CANDLEWAKE — NARRATIVE TRUTH

**Status:** derived from `docs/CANON.md`. Canon is law; this document adds detail
beneath it and may not contradict it.

**Audience:** writers, quest designers, dialogue implementers, QA. This is the
only document that states what is *objectively true* in the fiction. No other
document, and no character in the game, is permitted to be more correct than
this one.

**Rule of use.** If a line of dialogue would require a character to know
something not listed in their row of §C, the line is wrong. Not "risky" —
wrong. Rewrite it or move the information to a character who may hold it.

## A. What actually happened

### A.0 Calendar and clock conventions

- Dates are given in the Pall calendar where canon uses it (*12 Thaw 2231*) and
  in plain year otherwise.
- Ship-time is a 24-hour clock, four watches of six hours. Third watch is
  02:00–08:00.
- "Present hour" means the Chapter One window, 2237, third watch.

**Design note.** The 2229 index predates the 2231 casting by two years. This is
not an error. The Ledger's address space was built before the people in it were
killed. When the player eventually understands the ordering of those two dates,
that *is* the reveal: Kest Harbour was not a disaster that produced an archive,
it was an archive that required a disaster.

**Why this person.** The index cannot be a member of the set it indexes — an
address inside the address space is a recursion the Vestibule could not close.
The Vestibule therefore needed someone born to Kest, keyed to its dialect,
kinship structure and natal registry conventions, who was demonstrably *not* in
Kest on 12 Thaw 2231. The resettlement lottery of 2216 produced exactly that
population. Fourteen candidates were screened. One fit.

**Hessa's condition through the chapter.** She is alive in Annex 3 for the whole
of it. The cradle removes memory at roughly one day of subjective material per
ninety minutes. Pulled at 05:00 (O1's earliest realistic intervention) she loses
about one day. Pulled at 07:30 she loses about three. Left to complete, she
loses four days — including every minute of the duct, the slate and Stray — and
wakes in Medical with no idea why anyone is upset. She is never killed in
Chapter One under any outcome. Under O2 she is transferred off Deck C for real,
smoothing completed, and is not available again until Chapter Four.

### A.3 The standing facts of the ship

- Declared keel mass: **4,410 t**. Trim solution actually flown: **6,120 t**.
  Difference: **1,710 t**.
- The 1,710 t is the Cold Registry. Breakdown, for anyone who has to make a
  readout look right: 91,400 tesserae at ~0.4 kg each is ~37 t of tile. The
  other ~1,673 t is vault shell, isolation lattice, independent power, dedicated
  atmosphere plant, and the ballast that makes a 37 t payload behave like a
  structural member. **The archive weighs forty-five times what the people in it
  weigh.** That ratio is a line of dialogue waiting to happen; give it to Cael.
- 340 crew, 212 awake. Deck C houses the awake rotation.
- Standing Order 9 is published. Standing Order 9-B exists in two copies aboard:
  Onwe's, in the Command safe on sealed Deck A, and Sabbat's, which he carries.
- The Ledger cannot be instantiated without a living index physically present
  and conscious at Sable Verge. **The player cannot be killed by anyone who
  understands the mission.** This is a hard structural fact and the player is
  permitted to deduce it in Chapter Three, at which point it stops being safety
  and starts being horror.

## D. Mutable vs fixed

### D.1 Objectively fixed — no player action changes these

1. The Pall, the Overcast, the dates 2171 / 2174 / 2231 / 2237.
2. Kest Harbour was sealed, live-cast over nineteen days, and burned. 91,400.
3. The player was born there, evacuated in 2216, indexed in 2229, smoothed by
   Ashkar, and shipped rather than recruited. **Nothing the player does makes
   this untrue, including never learning it.**
4. The Ledger requires a living index physically present at Sable Verge.
5. The keel numbers: 4,410 declared, 6,120 flown, 1,710 undeclared.
6. Hessa read the tell-tale, went to Stray at 02:50, and was detained at 03:04

**The load-bearing asymmetry.** The truth is fixed; the player's access to it is
not. A player can complete CANDLEWAKE having assembled a coherent, well-evidenced
and entirely wrong account (ending family 9). The game must never signal, in
that playthrough, that they were wrong. The correction lives outside the
playthrough — in a second one, or in the player talking to another player.
